# Modlist Evolution

Case studies showing how an existing Fallout 4 modlist was adapted, tested and maintained.

- [From Wasteland of Depravity 1.7.4 to NORA](wod-1.7.4-to-nora.md)

These documents are snapshots, not official updates or ready-to-install modlists. Their purpose is to show architectural choices, validation methods and what may be possible with careful compatibility work.
